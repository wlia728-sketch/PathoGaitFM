PathoGaitFMInput preparation

Prepare your angles

This model accepts harmonised joint angles, not raw sensing signals.

One normalised cycle at a time

Segment each gait cycle using the appropriate source pipeline. Resample to 101 points including both endpoints, then remove the duplicate final point. The CSV uses 100 rows per cycle and gait_percent from 0 to 99. Use a separate cycle_id for each cycle. Left and right angles must follow the same cycle conventions as the source preparation pipeline; changing the row count alone does not establish this.

Degrees and anatomical conventions

Columns follow the model’s harmonised channel map. Hip and pelvis X/Y/Z are anatomical joint-angle components, not arbitrary sensor or camera axes. X is the sagittal component. The per-source transformations are documented in docs/data_format.md and data/external/. Align signs, zero offsets and cycle phase before uploading; do not apply a source correction twice.

The 16 angle columns are right/left hip X/Y/Z, right/left knee X, right/left ankle X, and the six pelvis slots of the released 40-channel representation. Preserve the source’s pelvis-slot mapping; do not invent a missing side.

Missing angles

Omit unavailable columns, or leave an entire channel blank for a cycle. A measured zero is a valid angle; it is not a missing-data indicator. Missing time samples within an otherwise available channel are rejected. “All available angles” uses only the columns actually supplied; the other six options omit pelvis, hip, knee or ankle angles, retain hip + knee, or retain hip only. The same masks apply to both sides where available.

Outputs

Joint moments are normalised by body mass and returned in N·m/kg. Vertical ground reaction force is returned in body weights, and displayed as %BW. The default upload run uses 50 sampling steps, three random seeds, and the ALLDATA checkpoint. Source and severity use unknown-condition tokens. No measured kinetics are supplied at inference.
