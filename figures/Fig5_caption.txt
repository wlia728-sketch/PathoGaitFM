Fig. 5 | Conditional denoising network and masked inpainting. (a) One denoising pass. Patches of four time points across all channels are embedded as tokens and passed through the conditional Transformer blocks. Summed diffusion-step, source, cohort and severity embeddings form c, which modulates every block and the output head. The output head restores the channel-by-time layout and predicts v, the diffusion target. (b) Kinetic estimation. From Gaussian noise, the network in (a) denoises the hidden kinetic channels over 50 sampling steps, while the observed joint angles are re-noised to the current noise level and reinserted at each step. Saved estimates are aggregated to obtain vGRF and joint moments. Matrices and waveforms are schematic.
