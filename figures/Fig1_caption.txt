Fig. 1 | Estimating gait kinetics from available joint angles. Complete and partial inputs supply observed joint angles. Hatching marks unavailable angles; question marks denote unobserved kinetics. No measured kinetics are provided at inference. All channels are initialised as Gaussian noise. After each model update, observed angles are re-noised to the next sampling level and reinserted, while updated kinetic channels are retained. Sampling continues for 50 steps. Saved estimates are aggregated across steps and random seeds to obtain vGRF and sagittal joint moments. Icons indicate input sources and development cohorts. Waveforms are schematic. PD joint moments were not evaluated. vGRF, vertical ground reaction force; TD, typically developing; CP, cerebral palsy; PD, Parkinson's disease; IMU, inertial measurement unit; q, joint angle; M, joint moment; Fz, vGRF.
